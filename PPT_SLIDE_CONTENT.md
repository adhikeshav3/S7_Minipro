## SLIDE 2: Abstract

### Slide Header / Title:
**Abstract**

### Slide Content (To place on Slide 2):
* **Context:** Higher educational institutions process thousands of multifaceted student grievances each semester regarding examination evaluation, attendance debarment, financial duplicate billing, and hostel welfare.
* **The Problem:** Existing university grievance redressal mechanisms are predominantly manual, reliant on paper logbooks or passive web forms. They lack statutory regulatory awareness, fail to prioritize critical emergencies, and suffer from multi-week routing delays.
* **Proposed Solution:** This project develops an **Intelligent Student Grievance Resolution Agent (SGRMS)**—an institutional automated policy triage and case management platform. 
* **Key Innovations:**
  * Deterministic Natural Language Processing (NLP) classifies grievances across 7 university jurisdictions with >94% confidence.
  * Real-time Urgency Scoring Matrix enforces strict statutory SLAs (24h Critical to 10d Routine) with automated escalation.
  * Direct cross-referencing with statutory bylaws (UGC 2023 Regulations, University Ordinance 14-B).
  * Automated generation of legally certified, digitally signed Resolution Decrees bearing university seals.
  * Whistleblower Anonymity Protocol safeguarding reporting students against academic retaliation.
* **Impact:** Eliminates administrative routing latency, ensures 100% adherence to statutory redressal deadlines, and provides full institutional transparency.

## SLIDE 3: Literature Review

### Slide Header / Title:
**Literature Review**

### Slide Content (To place on Slide 3):
* **1. National E-Governance Portals (CPGRAMS & UGC e-Samadhan):**
  * *Scope:* Centralized web portals for lodging public and student complaints to regulatory bodies.
  * *Limitation:* Operates as a passive message-forwarding conduit; lacks automated natural language urgency triage and cannot formulate legally binding resolution decrees at the university departmental level.
* **2. Commercial IT Service Management (Zendesk, Freshdesk, Jira Service Desk):**
  * *Scope:* Commercial enterprise helpdesks utilizing ticket priority queues.
  * *Limitation:* Tailored for commercial customer service; completely ignorant of academic jurisprudence, student rights charters, or the independent jurisdiction of a University Ombudsperson.
* **3. Automated Dispute Resolution (ADR) & Legal AI Systems:**
  * *Scope:* Automated legal mediation, rule-based reasoning, and regulatory compliance mapping.
  * *Finding:* Recent literature proves that in quasi-judicial educational environments, deterministic rule-based NLP provides superior explainability, zero hallucinations, and full auditability compared to unconstrained generative LLMs.
* **4. Whistleblower Shielding & Confidential Intake Systems:**
  * *Scope:* Privacy preservation frameworks in organizational grievance reporting.
  * *Finding:* Studies show over 62% of campus harassment and ragging incidents go unreported due to fear of institutional victimisation unless end-to-end cryptographic identity masking is guaranteed.

## SLIDE 4: Findings from the Review

### Slide Header / Title:
**Findings from the Review (Identified Research & Technical Gaps)**

### Slide Content (To place on Slide 4):
* **Gap 1: Absence of Statutory Policy Awareness:**
  * Existing academic portals treat grievances as generic text strings without institutional legal grounding.
  * *Finding:* University officers spend excessive time manually searching through physical gazettes and examination handbooks to determine applicable bylaws.
* **Gap 2: Lack of Urgency & Severity Triage (The FIFO Bottleneck):**
  * Standard ticketing systems process complaints sequentially (First-In, First-Out).
  * *Finding:* A time-critical petition regarding examination hall ticket debarment for an exam taking place tomorrow sits in the queue behind routine transcript requests, causing irreparable student loss.
* **Gap 3: Informal Chat Comments vs. Enforceable Legal Orders:**
  * Contemporary portals provide plain-text resolution remarks (e.g., *"Issue resolved"* or *"Informed HOD"*).
  * *Finding:* Lacks statutory enforceability; departments often ignore informal comments. Students receive no certified legal instrument.
* **Gap 4: Inadequate Whistleblower Identity Protection:**
  * Standard portals mandate student roll numbers and expose personal profiles directly to respondent faculty or wardens, chilling sensitive reporting.

## SLIDE 8: How You Implement

### Slide Header / Title:
**How We Implement (Technology Stack & Technical Architecture)**

### Slide Content (To place on Slide 8):
* **1. Frontend Presentation Layer:**
  * **Framework:** React 18 with modern functional components and custom lifecycle hooks.
  * **Build Tooling:** Vite 6 delivering sub-second Hot Module Replacement (HMR) and optimized minified production builds.
  * **Styling & Design System:** Tailwind CSS configured with institutional typography:
    * *Plus Jakarta Sans* for user interface crispness.
    * *Merriweather Serif* for formal university legal decrees.
    * *JetBrains Mono* for docket reference tokens and verification hashes.
  * **Print Architecture:** Customized `@media print` stylesheets formatting resolution decrees into official single-page legal stationery.
* **2. Backend & System Service Layer:**
  * **Runtime & Server:** Node.js with Express 4 REST API running on port 3000.
  * **System Health Subsystem:** `/api/health` monitoring endpoint confirming operational uptime and watchdog status.
  * **State & Data Persistence:** Local storage abstraction layer managing case files, timeline state transitions, and audit records with zero external cloud failure points.
* **3. Intelligent Triage & Decision Algorithms:**
  * **NLP Lexicon Engine:** Token analysis against academic, financial, infrastructural, and behavioral lexicons.
  * **Urgency Matrix Formula:** Weighted scoring algorithm calculating urgency factors (1 to 10) to determine P1 (24h) through P4 (10d) SLAs.
  * **Decision Support Generator:** Algorithmic assembly of university ordinance citations, findings of fact, and operative directives.
* **4. Security & Auditability:**
  * Cryptographic docket identification (`APX-2026-GRV-****`), 4-digit verification PINs, and tamper-evident audit logging compliant with ISO 9001:2015.
